## Course outline

- **Topic 1 — Overview of Additive Manufacturing and 3D Scanning** (K1, K3, K6 · A1, A4)
  AM vs subtractive, the 7 ISO/ASTM process families, materials, benefits & trade-offs, case studies, 3D scanning and reverse engineering, design for AM in Fusion 360 — Labs 1–6
- **Topic 2 — Process Related Issues for AM** (K2, K4 · A2, A3)
  Printer anatomy, slicing, bed adhesion (skirt/brim/raft), supports, extrusion & temperature diagnostics, filaments, hybrid metal/polymer parts — Labs 7–10
- **Topic 3 — Process Improvement for AM** (K5, K7 · A5, A6)
  Infill & wall optimisation, surface quality (layer height, ironing, fuzzy skin), support strategy, post-processing chains, business impact and sustainability — Labs 11–16

## The 16 labs

Design (Fusion 360): extrude keychain token · sweep paper clip · loft plant pot · revolve funnel · 0.2 mm tolerance container & lid · 3D scan a real object.
Process (Cura + FDM): install & slice · skirt vs brim vs raft · supports for overhangs · filament diagnostics.
Optimisation: perimeters & infill · infill patterns · layer-height ladder · ironing · fuzzy skin · support removal & sanding.

Each lab folder is self-contained: `labs/lab-NN-*/README.md` + the STL files it needs (`cube10.stl`, `twist.stl`, `open vase.stl`, and an original `token.stl` generated by the build pipeline).

## Rebuilding the courseware

```bash
bash .claude/skills/courseware-build/build/build_courseware.sh   # PPT + LP + LG (+PDFs, TOCs)
python3 .claude/skills/courseware-build/build/build_labs.py      # regenerate labs/
```

All artifacts are generated from one content source (`.claude/skills/courseware-build/build/course_data.py` and `data_domain1..3.py`), so the deck, Lesson Plan, Learner Guide and labs can never drift apart.
